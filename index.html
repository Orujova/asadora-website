Our Craft

Slow-made, in small batches

ASADORA candles are poured by hand in Baku, in batches small enough to watch over every wick. We choose natural waxes and clean fragrance so the light burns soft, even, and true.

Sculptural forms, considered scent, and packaging made to be kept — an object that stays long after the flame is gone.
